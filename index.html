Product Requirements Document (PRD): AI Voice Agent

Status: Draft for review and approval

1. Vision & Positioning

Vision: To be the leading Arabic-first voice platform that gets things done (books, collects, sells, resolves) rather than one that merely "answers" the customer.

Positioning statement: A voice agent that speaks your customer's dialect, works inside your systems, and keeps your data in your country.

Competitive equation: Global competitors are technically strong in English but weak in dialects, local integrations, and data sovereignty. We combine all three, then add a layer of guaranteed outcomes (pricing and measurement based on results delivered).

3. Expanded Feature Tree

Tier 1 — Core (MVP)

Already in your plan (retained): Sub-500ms latency, Smart Barge-in, dialects + MSA + English, API & Webhooks, session management.

Additions essential to MVP success:

  • Semantic Turn Detection: doesn't interrupt someone who is thinking, and doesn't wait too long after they finish.
  • Number & name accuracy: pronounces and understands IDs, phone numbers, and dates (Hijri/Gregorian), spells Arabic names, and performs a verbal read-back before any action.
  • Voicemail/IVR detection + DTMF support (inbound and outbound calls).
  • Warm Transfer: instant summary delivered to the human agent before pickup.
  • Call recording + transcription + automatic summary for every call.
  • Telephony: SIP Trunking and local numbers through licensed telecom providers.
  • Agent Studio: no-code builder + prompt editing, Sandbox testing, versioning with rollback.
  • Disclosure & consent: automatic notice of recording and that the caller is speaking with AI.

Tier 2 — Advanced

Already in your plan: Persistent Memory, Sentiment Analysis, Proactive Responses, WhatsApp triggers.

Additions:

  • Multi-Agent Orchestration: specialized agents (reception → sales → support → collections) hand off to one another seamlessly.
  • Safe action execution (Tool Calling): confirmation before sensitive operations (payment, cancellation, data changes) and permission limits per agent.
  • RAG with citations: every answer is tied to a knowledge-base passage; if no answer exists, the agent says "I don't know" and escalates.
  • Agent Copilot for human staff: real-time suggestions, summarization, and automatic CRM updates during their calls.
  • Live monitoring dashboard: listen in, whisper to the agent, or take over the call instantly.
  • A/B testing of prompts and voices, with impact measured on outcomes.
  • Smart outbound calling: campaigns, best time to call (respects prayer times and working hours), smart retries, and compliance with do-not-disturb lists.
